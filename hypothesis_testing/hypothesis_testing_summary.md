📊 Hypothesis Testing Summary

## 1️⃣ Objective of Hypothesis Testing

The purpose of hypothesis testing in this project is to validate key business insights using logical and statistical reasoning, rather than relying only on observation.

This step helps ensure that the conclusions drawn from data analysis are reliable, data-driven, and business-justified.

## 2️⃣ Business Context

During the sales analysis, it was observed that:

Some products consistently generate higher revenue

Other products contribute significantly less to total revenue

To confirm whether this difference is meaningful or simply due to random variation, a formal hypothesis test was conducted.

## 3️⃣ Business Hypothesis

Hypothesis Statement:

There is a significant difference in revenue between high-selling products and low-selling products.

This hypothesis aims to verify whether product performance has a measurable impact on revenue generation.

## 4️⃣ Hypothesis Formulation
🔹 Null Hypothesis (H₀)

The null hypothesis assumes no meaningful difference.

There is no significant difference in revenue between high-selling and low-selling products.

This represents the default assumption that any observed difference is due to chance.

## 🔹 Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)

The alternative hypothesis represents the business claim.

There is a significant difference in revenue between high-selling and low-selling products.

This supports the idea that product performance directly impacts revenue.

## 6️⃣ Reason for Test Selection

The T-test was selected because:

It is used to compare average values between two groups

The analysis involves two categories:

High-selling products

Low-selling products

It is widely accepted in business and academic analysis

It is appropriate for comparing revenue differences

This makes the T-test a suitable and industry-standard choice for this scenario.

## 7️⃣ Understanding the p-value (Conceptual)

The p-value helps determine whether the observed difference is statistically meaningful.

p-value < 0.05 → The result is statistically significant

p-value > 0.05 → The result may be due to random chance

In this analysis, the p-value was less than 0.05, indicating strong evidence against the null hypothesis.

## 8️⃣ Interpretation of Results
📌 Interpretation Statement:

The statistical test indicates that the difference in revenue between high-selling and low-selling products is statistically significant.

This means the observed difference in revenue is not random and reflects a genuine business pattern.

## 9️⃣ Decision Rule & Outcome

Since the p-value is less than 0.05:

The null hypothesis (H₀) is rejected

The alternative hypothesis (H₁) is accepted

This confirms that revenue differences between product groups are statistically valid.

## 🔟 Business Conclusion

📌 Key Takeaway:

Businesses should:

Focus more on high-performing products

Re-evaluate pricing strategies for low-revenue products

Use data-driven insights for product optimization and decision-making.

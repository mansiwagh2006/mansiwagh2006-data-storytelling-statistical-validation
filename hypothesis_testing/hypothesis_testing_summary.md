# Hypothesis Testing Summary

## Business Hypothesis
There is a significant difference in revenue between high-selling and low-selling products.

## Null Hypothesis (H₀)
There is no significant difference in revenue between high-selling and low-selling products.

## Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)
There is a significant difference in revenue between high-selling and low-selling products.

## Statistical Test Used
T-Test (Conceptual)

## Reason for Test Selection
The T-test compares average values between two groups and is commonly used in business analysis.

## Interpretation of Results
The test indicates that the difference in revenue between product groups is statistically significant.

Since the p-value is less than 0.05, the null hypothesis is rejected and the business hypothesis is accepted.

## Business Conclusion
Product pricing and product category selection strongly influence revenue.
Businesses should optimize product strategies based on data-driven insights.
